Civitas Alpha 0.28.0 — Persistent Chunk Territory
Target: Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1 + Forge 52.1.20 + Java 21

Added:
- Persisted overworld chunk claims in CivitasSavedData; old worlds without claim data load with an empty claim set.
- Founding automatically claims the founding chunk, which cannot be unclaimed; existing founded worlds migrate to that chunk on load.
- /civitas territory reports influence and claim capacity.
- /civitas territory claim and unclaim manage the chunk where the owner stands.
- /civitas territory show emits temporary particle markers along claimed chunk edges.
- Explicit-position construction and blueprint previews require the anchor chunk to be claimed and inside the influence radius.
- Claim capacity scales with influence (minimum 2, maximum 16 chunks); new claims must touch an existing claim by an edge and remain within the influence radius.

Limitations:
- Claims are for the single player settlement in the overworld; no rival ownership or contested borders yet.
- Claims are not general block protection and do not stop natural changes, explosions, mobs, or other mods.
- Building validation still checks the anchor position, not the complete structure footprint.
- Boundary particles are temporary and shown at the player's current height.

Validation:
- GitHub Actions build/test/JAR packaging for the exact release commit is not yet confirmed.
- Manual gameplay and multiplayer checks have not been performed here.
